## Originality check (guardrail §7.6)
Deliberately avoided look-alikes: no turtles (TMNT), no red-panda or panda master (Kung Fu Panda),
no blocky toy style (Ninjago), no animal-hero pajama look (PJ Masks). No weapons anywhere.

## Kiko — Hero ninja
- Age 6 · curious, brave-but-careful, giggly
- Look: round face, big eyes, teal gi, sunshine-yellow headband, little star badge
- Catchphrase: **"Ninja eyes ON!"** (opens every Ninja Move: the cue to stop and look)

## Sensei Hoot — Sensei
- Wise, gentle snowy owl · round spectacles · lavender robe
- Teaching style: asks a question, waits, then shows the move calmly (this fits Format B: "What should Ninja do?")
- Signature line: **"A true ninja stays calm and gets a grown-up."** (builds guardrail §7.7 into the character)

## Zip — Sidekick
- Tiny glowing firefly with goggles
- Role: **lights up = stop and think.** It's a visual cue that works without reading, which suits the hook rule
- Comic trait: zooms too fast and bonks into things (safe slapstick that gets laughs)

## Signature chant
**"Stop! Think! Ninja safe!"** Three beats, so kids can clap it or act it out.
